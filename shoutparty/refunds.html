Refund policy — Shout Party

Effective date · 4 October 2026

Versão em português (Brasil)

This policy covers the optional word packs sold inside Shout Party (“the App”). The App itself is free.

1. What you are buying

Word packs are digital content: extra sets of words and phrases for the game. Each pack is a one-time purchase. It is not a subscription and never renews. Once bought, a pack stays available to the Google account that bought it, on any Android device where that account is signed in to Google Play.

2. Who sells it

Purchases are made through Google Play. Google processes the payment, charges any applicable taxes and issues your receipt. We never see or store your card or payment details.

3. Your right to a refund

You can ask for a refund of any word pack within 7 days of buying it, for any reason. You do not have to explain why.

This applies to everyone, wherever you live, and comes on top of any rights you have under the consumer law of your country (see Section 7).

When a pack is refunded, it is removed from your account and becomes locked in the App again.

4. How to ask

Within 48 hours of the purchase the quickest route is Google Play itself:

  • Open play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.
  • Find the order and choose Report a problem or Request a refund.

After 48 hours, or if Google Play declines, email us at contact@sepulka.cc with:

  • the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is on your receipt email), and
  • the name of the pack.

We reply within 5 working days. Approved refunds are returned by Google Play to the original payment method; how long that takes depends on your payment provider.

5. After 7 days

After 7 days we refund when something is actually wrong, for example:

  • the pack was charged but never unlocked, and “Restore purchases” in Settings does not fix it;
  • the pack does not work in your language as described;
  • you were charged twice for the same pack.

Write to contact@sepulka.cc with the order number and a short description.

6. Purchases made by children

If a child bought a pack without your permission, contact us with the order number and we will refund it. You can require a password or biometric check for every Google Play purchase in the Play Store app under Settings → Purchase verification.

7. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the consumer law of your country that cannot be waived by contract, including the legal guarantee of conformity for digital content in the European Union.
